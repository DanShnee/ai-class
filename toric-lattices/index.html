The core idea

Two lattices,
one conversation.

Toric geometry translates geometry into lattice data. The two lattices describe complementary ways of probing the same torus, while the fan records how that torus is compactified or enlarged into a toric variety.

01

Characters live in M

A point m ∈ M is a character: a monomial map from the torus T to 𝔾m. You can think of M as the lattice of exponent vectors for Laurent monomials.

m ↦ χm
02

Cocharacters live in N

A point n ∈ N is a one-parameter subgroup 𝔾m → T. The lattices are dual: composing χm with n gives t ↦ t⟨m,n⟩, so their pairing is always an integer.

N = Hom(M, ℤ)
03

The fan lives in Nℝ

Rays give torus-invariant boundary divisors. Each cone σ selects the characters that remain regular near its boundary, producing an affine chart. Shared faces tell those charts how to glue.

About

A small toric geometry workbench.

The columns of B describe a basis of M in ambient coordinates. The dual basis is the inverse transpose B−T, characterized by ⟨mᵢ,nⱼ⟩ = δᵢⱼ.
